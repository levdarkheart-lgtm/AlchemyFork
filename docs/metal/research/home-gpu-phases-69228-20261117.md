Main/hero/ordinary kinds have separate slots, probe faces share their kind's slot and retain their actual face. Failed attempts do not publish success. Legacy timing remains off; existing PBR/water/signature scans remain legacy-only. Each numeric row retains explicit source frame/view and the same observation ID for lossy joins:
